# Apple→Plate observation re-probe v1: results (TASK-061)

**Outcome O-LOOK-RAW.** The protocol (§8) defines this row as: L-raw passes and L-E0 does not.

- **A reset-independent 8-command look, taken before the decision frame, makes the apple
  position and the expert's first reset-dependent command readable from the existing 112 px
  onboard camera.** Raw pixels read them far beyond the prior, and they do so on the 111 roots
  where the wrist hid the apple at reset as well.
- **The frozen E0 encoder does not expose that information.** L-E0 fails the unchanged bars. A
  random-init encoder of the same architecture reads the post-look frame better than E0 does
  (reported only).
- **Two more hypotheses pass:** LH-raw (the same look, 224 px) and O-raw (the fixed overview
  camera at reset). They do not change the row, because the rows are ordered by the size of the
  change to the robot. The look at 112 px comes first.

**What this is and is not.**
- This is an offline **readability** result: kernel-ridge readouts, 10-fold CV, 190 roots.
- It shows that the information is present in the decision frame and can be linearly or
  RBF-read.
- It is **not** a controller, and it says nothing about closed-loop behaviour.

**Owner ruling, recorded 2026-09-25T23:28Z, verbatim in substance:**

> Accepted as proposed. It is a **reproduction mismatch caused by the renderer's first-render
> artifact**, not a code regression. No row or verdict changes. The results document records the
> differing fields and the max |Δ| (5.7e-9), states that the T2 counts, flags and readings are

> `apple_info_ceiling_v1_results.md`, and it changes no number or verdict. The PR 3 reviewer
> independently checks the cause claim.

**The cause, with evidence.**
- TASK-059's runner never warmed up its 448 px renderer, so its first render (root 48000) was
  cold. This run warms every renderer up (the owner's ruling before the run, protocol §5).
- Re-rendering root 48000 at 448 px with a fresh renderer, the cold first render differs from
  the warm second render by **1 pixel of 1 level**. The second and third renders are identical.
- Two other explanations were ruled out. Box-downsampling in a batch or frame by frame gives
  identical arrays, and their Gram matrices are also identical.
- **Consequence for TASK-059:** its (v-b) and raw-448 figures carry this 1-pixel artifact on one
  root. The effect on any reported number is at most 5.7e-9.

## 6. Reading (interpretation, not measurement)

1. **Occlusion at reset was the binding constraint on the onboard camera, and a
   reset-independent look removes it.**
   - Same camera, same resolution, same readout: the look moves raw-pixel T1 from 1.853 cm to
     0.469 cm and T2 from 114/190 to 180/190.
   - The gain is largest on the roots hidden at reset (−1.624 cm).
   - TASK-059's visible stratum had suggested that resolution might also bind. At the post-look
     pose it does not: the apple covers a median of 12 px there (5 px at reset), and 112 px
     suffices.
2. **The look carries no reset information of its own.** Commands and the post-look state are
   identical on every root. With the apple hidden, every readout falls to the prior. What is read
   is the apple.
3. **Frozen E0 is the weak link.** It reads the post-look frame worse than raw pixels (+0.803 cm)
   and no better than a random-init encoder. The same frozen E0 does read the overview frame
   (O-E0, reported only). So E0 can represent a visible apple, but it discards it in the post-look
   onboard view. That is consistent with TASK-059's finding that E0 discards position information.
   **This is a representation problem, not an information problem.**
4. **Resolution helps a little once the wrist is clear, and not at all before.** LH is better than
   L by 0.084 cm. H is no better than the anchor.
5. **The overview camera reads best of all** (0.183 cm, 185/190). It sits last in the preregistered
   order because the G1 has no scene camera; on the robot it would be a hardware/workspace change.
6. **What this does not show.**
   - Whether a policy trained on look-prefixed demonstrations would issue the right first command.
     That is closed-loop behaviour, and it is untested.
   - Anything about the post-grasp carry failure (TASK-057 §5).
   - That these readouts transfer beyond the 190 train and val roots.

## 7. Recommended next task (a recommendation; the task owner chooses)

The preregistered row O-LOOK-RAW implies: **a look-prefix corpus at the 112 px onboard camera,
prereg-gated, plus separately preregistered encoder/representation work.** Concretely, in order:

1. **Encoder work first, because it is cheap and needs no new data.** It should be preregistered
   on the existing frames plus re-rendered post-look frames of the 170 train roots only.
   - The question: can an encoder be obtained whose frozen features pass the same probe on the
     post-look frame, as raw pixels do?
   - Candidates: fine-tuning E0 on look-prefixed frames, or a different pretrained encoder.
   - Any new encoder needs its own capacity-controlled comparison.
   - The random-init result (0.926 T2) is a warning: a strong floor must be part of that
     protocol.
2. **Then a look-prefix corpus collection.** Every episode starts with the same 8-command look,
   and the 112 px onboard camera is kept.
   - It is prereg-gated.
   - It would reuse the frozen seeds or plan, and would reserve test and cohorts as before.
   - No control formulation should be preregistered until an encoder passes this probe on that
     corpus's decision frames.
3. **The overview camera and 224 px arms passed too.** They are fallbacks, not the recommendation.
   - Overview: an external camera is a hardware change on the robot.
   - 224 px: needs an encoder at a new input size.
   - If the 224 px route is ever taken, the renderer warm-up must carry over to the collector,
     because a cold first render would otherwise enter the corpus.

## 8. Caveats kept in the record

- **P3 is stricter than §5 requires.** It also requires the 224 px replica's joint state to equal
  the 224 px primary's. The calibration measured it the same way (190/190), and here it held on
  190/190. It could only have demoted LH, not promoted it.
- **The manifest's `warm_up_rule` quotes the first scratch finding ("1 px, 1 level").** The PR #54
  reviewer saw up to 5 levels on one root at 224 px overview, a camera no arm uses. P5 is one
  check per run, recorded per root.
- **Out-of-fold predictions come from ten models**, so the Wilson intervals assume independence
  only approximately (as in TASK-059). Kernel-ridge values are not bit-reproducible across
  machines (BLAS).
- **Only the preregistered readout families were tested:** linear and RBF kernel ridge on n = 190.
- **The look was selected on visibility, on the same 190 roots.** This is disclosed in the
  protocol. It used no target and no readout.
- **The T2 targets for L and LH are the expert's command at the post-look pose.** The sign equals
  the reset sign on 189/190 roots.
