# Apple→Plate token dynamics v1: results (TASK-066)

**Outcome WM-TOK-DYNAMICS.** Every model seed (0, 1, 2) passes G1–G5 at both gated horizons
(h = 8 and h = 16). The encoded 4 × 4 grid meets the T1 bar, so G5 tested prediction. The
abandonment clause does not fire.

**Read this with its four caveats; they are stated here first, plainly:**
1. **The budget did not saturate.** 9 of the 12 models selected one of their last two validation
   points, and **every N model did** (N-s1-B, N-s2-A and N-s2-B selected the final update, 30 000).
   The no-action baseline N was probably still improving when training stopped. An under-trained
   N makes W look better on **G3** (W beats N) and on **G1 (iii)** (W keeps more rank than N). The
   owner's budget ruling (30 000 updates) was made to avoid exactly this bias, and it did not
   fully avoid it.
2. **Some margins are narrow.**
   - **G2 at h = 8**: the upper bound of W / copy-last is 0.790, 0.792 and 0.791 against the bar
     of 0.8.
   - **G5 for seed 2 at h = 8**: the upper bound of the ratio to B-occ is 0.581 against 0.6.
3. **The token latent does not lose less rank than the CLS latent did.** W's effective-rank ratio
   here is 0.325–0.344, against 0.365–0.399 on TASK-065's CLS. G1 passes because its bar is
   calibrated at 0.16 (§6 of the protocol), not because tokens avoid rank loss better.
4. **The claim is narrow.** It holds on the train split only, on 170 cross-fitted train sessions of
   one corpus, at 0.4 s and 0.8 s horizons, with a static apple. It is **not a test-split result**,
   and it makes **no control claim**.

**What this is and is not.**
- **A world-model test only, offline, on stored frames.** It is not a controller. **No control
  formulation was preregistered, and none is implied.** The TASK-054 (CEM) and TASK-057
  (behaviour-cloning) clauses still hold.
- **Learned Apple→Plate is still 0 successes.**
- **The test split of `apple-look-v1` was never decoded** (`test_split_decoded: false`). Cohorts C
  and D were never simulated; nothing was simulated. `exemption_spent` stays `false`.

Protocol: [`apple_token_dynamics_v1.md`](apple_token_dynamics_v1.md) (PR #74; runner PR #75).
Results manifest: `benchmarks/manifests/apple-token-dynamics-v1-results.json`, generated by

- Brackets are 95 % intervals. For latent-MSE ratios: a session-clustered bootstrap (10 000

## 5. Reading (interpretation, not measurement)

1. **By the preregistered gates, the predictor learns action-conditioned latent dynamics on this
   latent and keeps the apple readable.** On every seed, at 0.4 s and 0.8 s, it removes about
   22–36 % of copy-last's latent error, beats an equally budgeted no-action predictor by about
   9–13 %, costs about 71–119 % more error with wrong or zero actions, keeps a third of the encoded
   rank and about 82–84 % of the spread, and reads the apple to 0.75–0.93 cm (upper bounds ≤ 1.03 cm), within 0.21–0.34 cm
   of the encoded grid.
2. **The pass is not strong.** G2 at h = 8 and G5 for seed 2 at h = 8 pass by less than 0.02 on
   their bounds. The N baseline was probably under-trained, which favours W on G3 and G1 (iii).
3. **Rank loss is not solved.** Predictions span about 17–18 effective dimensions of 53. The gate
   calls this non-collapse because the calibrated reference (the same recipe on disjoint pilot
   data) loses rank the same way.
4. **What it does not show**: control, closed-loop behaviour, horizons beyond 16 as a gated
   claim, a moving apple (the apple is static on every root read), the test split, other
   encoders, other grids, or that pretraining matters (random-init tokens also read the apple,
   TASK-064).

## 6. Recommended next task (a recommendation; the task owner chooses)

Per protocol §11, the WM-TOK-DYNAMICS row implies a separately preregistered **held-out
confirmation on the corpus's test split** (40 episodes, never decoded until then). To make that
confirmation informative, it should:
- **fix the saturation caveat first**: a budget or schedule under which N (and W) reach a
  plateau, established on train/val before the freeze;
- keep the gates and the calibrated bars of this task unchanged, so the confirmation tests the
  same claim;
- be read as a confirmation only: G2 at h = 8 and G5 on seed 2 were near their bars here.

Alternatively or afterwards, a harder world-model test (later task phases, a moving apple,
longer horizons). **No control formulation is recommended or implied.** Control use needs its own
preregistration and must answer the TASK-054 and TASK-057 clauses.

## 7. Caveats kept in the record

- **Caveats 1–4 above.**
- **The G1 bars came from a 32-root pilot** and were set after seeing pilot behaviour of the same
  architecture (protocol §6.3). G1 (iii) uses a 256-direction projected approximation.
- **G5's 0.5 cm margin is uncalibrated** (it could not be calibrated under the leakage rules).
- **Pre-freeze reads of the corpus** (protocol §6.4 and §14): an encoded-only ceiling pre-check on
  the 190 train + val roots, and a runner smoke with noise targets. Neither touched a predicted
  latent of the gated run or the test split.
- **The 4 × 4 pooling costs 0.04–0.09 cm** of readability against full tokens (protocol §6.4).
- **MPS training is not bit-reproducible.** The seeds and budget are fixed, not the bits.
- **One corpus, one encoder, one grid, 170 cross-fitted train sessions, a static apple.**
- **Learned Apple→Plate is still 0 successes.**
