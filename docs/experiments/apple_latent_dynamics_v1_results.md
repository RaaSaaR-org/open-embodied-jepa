# Apple→Plate latent dynamics v1: results (TASK-065)

**Outcome WM-NO-DYNAMICS. The abandonment clause fires.**

**The row name overstates the failure.** The predictor did learn action-conditioned latent
dynamics:
- **G2, G3 and G4 pass on all three seeds at both horizons.** It beats copy-last and an equally
  trained no-action predictor, and it is strongly action-sensitive.
- The run **fails the preregistered no-collapse rank gate and the apple-readability gate.** It
  is not a run that "learns no dynamics".
  - **G1 fails on every seed on its effective-rank criterion alone.** The ratio is about
    0.37–0.40 against an uncalibrated bar of 0.5. The std-ratio and collapsed-fraction parts
    pass.
  - **G5 fails.** Apple readability degrades through prediction beyond the preregistered margin.

**Which row, and why.** Protocol §10 takes the first matching row.
- **WM-APPLE-LOST** needs G1–G4 to pass on at least two seeds. G1 fails on all three, so it does
  not match.
- **WM-NO-DYNAMICS** ("otherwise") is therefore the row.
- **Had G1 passed, G5 alone would have mapped to WM-APPLE-LOST.** Both rows fire the same
  abandonment clause, so the consequence is the same either way.

**What this is and is not.**
- **A world-model test only, offline, on stored frames.** It is not a controller, and it says
  nothing about closed-loop behaviour. **No control formulation was preregistered, and none is
  implied.** The TASK-054 (CEM) and TASK-057 (behaviour-cloning) clauses still hold.
- **Learned Apple→Plate is still 0 successes.**
- **The test split of `apple-look-v1` was never decoded** (`test_split_decoded: false`).
- **Cohorts C and D were never simulated.** Nothing was simulated.
- **`exemption_spent` stays `false`.**

Protocol: [`apple_latent_dynamics_v1.md`](apple_latent_dynamics_v1.md) (PR #69; runner PR #70;
amendment PR #71). Results manifest: `benchmarks/manifests/apple-latent-dynamics-v1-results.json`,

## 4. Reading (interpretation, not measurement)

1. **The predictor learned action-conditioned latent dynamics, by the preregistered latent-error
   tests.**
   - At h = 8 and h = 16 it removes about 25–35 % of copy-last's error.
   - It beats an equally trained no-action predictor by about 9–12 %.
   - Wrong or zero actions cost it about 80–140 % more error.
   - All of this is consistent across three seeds and tight in its intervals.
2. **The predictions span far fewer dimensions than the encoded latents.**
   - The effective rank is about 15–16 against about 40, a ratio of about 0.37–0.40.
   - The predictions still keep about 81–85 % of the per-dimension spread.
   - Regression to a conditional mean would lower the rank. A no-action model does so even more
     (about 0.28–0.33).
   - **Under the preregistered G1 bar (0.5) this is collapse, and the rule is applied as
     written.** The bar was fixed without calibration. It is not revisited after the fact.
3. **The apple survives prediction only partially.**
   - Predicted latents read the apple to about 0.9–1.2 cm at h = 8–16, against 0.68–0.83 cm for
     the encoded frames.
   - That is far better than copy-last or N (about 2.1–2.4 cm), and inside the absolute
     1.5 cm bar.
   - But it is not within the preregistered 0.5 cm non-inferiority margin at h = 16 on any
     seed, and the ratio-to-B-occ bar fails on 5 of the 6 seed-horizon pairs.
   - Longer rollouts degrade further (about 1.5–2.0 cm at h = 32–64).
4. **So the row is WM-NO-DYNAMICS, and its label says more than the evidence does.** The latent
   error gates (G2–G4) all passed. What failed is the rank criterion of G1 and the apple
   criteria of G5. The first-matching-row rule maps "G1 fails on every seed" to WM-NO-DYNAMICS,
   and this document does not re-label it.
5. **Cautions.**
   - The G1 rank bar was fixed without calibration.
   - Checkpoints were selected late (updates 8000–10 000), so the budget may not have saturated.
   - The no-action model N has an even lower rank ratio (0.28–0.33).
   - On the apple readout, W beats copy-last and N by about 1.0–1.5 cm. That comparison is
     reported only.

## 5. The abandonment clause (protocol §10), as it fires

- **What closes:** *an action-conditioned LeWM-family predictor on frozen, externally pretrained
  pooled (CLS) latents on `apple-look-v1`*. No further predictor variant with frozen pooled
  pretrained latents on this corpus is preregistered without new evidence of a different kind.
  That covers history length, action chunking, step embeddings, loss weighting, capacity,
  residual parameterisation and budget.
- **Its scope is exactly what the protocol names, and nothing wider.** The following are **not
  abandoned**:
  - the LeWM backend;
  - DINOv2 as an encoder;
  - patch-token latents;
  - the product goal;
  - the corpus. It stays sealed, and its test split is still unread.
- **Recorded as untested, not refuted:**
  - patch-token latents (P-tok) as a world-model latent;
  - a trainable encoder initialised from the pretrained one;
  - other encoders.
- **Context, recorded and not preregistered:** the fixed `overview` camera (TASK-061 O-raw) is
  still the observation change on record. It is a hardware/workspace change, and the owner's
  decision.

## 6. Recommended next task (a recommendation; the task owner chooses)

**Recommended: a preregistered patch-token latent predictor on `apple-look-v1`**, in the style of
DINO-WM. It would predict the frozen DINOv2 final patch tokens (P-tok).
- **Why this one.** P-tok read the apple better than the pooled CLS: 0.394 cm on TASK-063's
  roots, and 0.456 cm [0.406, 0.528] on this corpus in TASK-064. It is also a different line from
  the one the clause closes.
- **What to calibrate before the freeze.** Its training budget and any rank/collapse bar should be
  set on train/val, on a disclosed pilot. Here an uncalibrated rank bar decided the row while the
  latent-error gates passed.
- **The alternative on record** is the observation change (the overview camera), which is a
  hardware/workspace decision.
- **No further variant of the pooled-CLS predictor** (the clause).
- **No control formulation is recommended or implied.** The TASK-054 and TASK-057 clauses hold.

## 7. Caveats kept in the record

- **G1's 0.5 rank bar and G5's 0.5 cm margin were fixed without a pilot on the corpus.** They
  decided the row, and they are not revised.
- **The amendment went beyond §12's "runner mechanics" limit.** It redefined a §9 guard, made
  by owner ruling under §15.5, before any outcome data existed (§1).
- **One corpus, one encoder, 170 cross-fitted train sessions.** The results are not test-split
  results.
- **MPS training is not bit-reproducible.** The seeds and budget are fixed, not the bits.
- **The apple is static over every horizon read here** (0 roots moved more than 1 mm). E-post
  therefore measures whether prediction keeps a static object readable while the arm moves. It
  does not measure object dynamics.
- **Late checkpoint selection** (updates 8000–10 000) suggests an unsaturated budget. Budget is
  among the variants the clause closes on this corpus.
- **P-cls is not detectably different from raw pixels as a readout** (TASK-064). Nothing here
  separates pretraining from pixels for dynamics.
- **Two items the reviewers left non-blocking, not fixed:**
  - protocol §11 and the runner docstring still name the run-1 paths; the manifest's run command
    named run-2;
  - the amendment kept about 4 GB of decoded episodes in memory. Peak RSS was 6.38 GB
    (5.94 GiB).
