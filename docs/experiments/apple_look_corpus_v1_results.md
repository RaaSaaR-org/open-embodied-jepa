## 5. Reading (interpretation, not measurement)

1. **The corpus is what the protocol specified.** The look prefix is reset-independent on every
   root. The collector succeeded from the post-look pose at a rate in the range of `apple-wide-v1`,
   and every acceptance check held with margin. The test split was untouched.
2. **TASK-063's P-cls result replicated on fresh resets.**
   - Its T1 median, 0.550 cm, is close to TASK-063's 0.538 cm.
   - It beats its floor by a similar margin: −0.298 cm, against −0.316 cm in TASK-063.
   - This addresses one TASK-063 caveat: the result is not specific to the 190 roots on which the
     look was chosen.
3. **The caveats that remain are unchanged.**
   - **P-cls is still not detectably different from raw pixels:** −0.034 cm [−0.125, 0.054]. No
     equivalence margin was preregistered.
   - **Random-init features still read the apple.**
     - R-tok met every bar again (0.572 cm, 181/190).
     - R-cls read the apple to 0.848 cm with 172/190, but missed the T3 bar here (upper bound
       0.640). In TASK-063 it met every bar.
     - So "both floors meet the bars on their own" holds on these roots for the tokens only.
       The pooled floor is near the T3 bar in both runs.
   - **The sign advantage of P-cls over R-cls is not significant by itself** (McNemar
     p = 0.210). The preregistered floor rule compares point accuracies, and it was met.
   - **One encoder, one input size, one floor seed.** Floor-seed variance is not measured.
   - **The cause is not identified.** Pretraining data, width and input size all changed at once
     in TASK-063, and nothing here separates them.
4. **What this does not show.**
   - Whether a world model trained on `apple-look-v1`, with this encoder or any other, keeps the
     apple through prediction.
   - Whether a controller could use it.
   - Anything closed-loop.
   - Anything about the test split, which was not read.

## 7. Caveats kept in the record

- **Render nondeterminism (protocol §16).**
  - Physics is bit-identical across runs, but a few mid-episode frames can differ by 1–2 px
    between runs.
  - A re-collection would therefore reproduce the physics, not necessarily the dataset hash.
  - The accepted corpus is these bytes: manifest `81d760d1…db64`.
- **A6 and A7 include the 8 look transitions per root episode** (protocol §16).
- **The Wilson intervals assume independence only approximately.** The out-of-fold predictions
  come from ten readouts, as in TASK-059 to TASK-063.
- **Kernel-ridge values are not bit-reproducible across machines** (BLAS). The gate ran on the
  machine TASK-061 to TASK-063 used.
- **Non-blocking review items left open** (PR #66 re-review):
  - the gate script's digest is checked at finalize, not recorded at the moment it is loaded;
  - a non-guard exception inside preflight would write no report;
  - the frozen `check_pins` branch of `finalize` has no unit test.
  - None of these arose in this run: `finalize` re-checked all 28 pins and they matched.
- **Oracle actions.** As TASK-048 said of `apple-wide-v1`: the actions come from a privileged
  oracle and its perturbations, the left arm is never excited, and outcome labels are scorer stage
  flags.
